Daily Itinerary

DAY 01

8 Feb · Arrival & Seafood Evening

✈️ Arrival → 🏨 Relax → 🦐 Seafood → 💆 Massage → 🌙 Rest

15:40 - Arrival in Da Nang

Immigration & Baggage. Meet driver. Transfer to Rosamia Hotel (Tour Agent Transfer: ~3M VND).

17:15 - Check-in & Rest

Rest at the hotel. No activities planned to let parents recover from the journey.

19:00 - Seafood Dinner

📍 Ăn Thôi Hải Sản (290 Võ Nguyên Giáp)

Recommendations: Prawns, Shellfish, Steamed Fish, Grilled Squid, Vegetables, Rice. (No beef, less spicy).

20:30 - Massage & Nail

📍 Chang Massage & Nail. Perfect for post-flight relaxation. Return to hotel by 22:00.

DAY 02

9 Feb · Ba Na Hills & City Highlights

🏔️ Mountain → 🌉 Golden Bridge → 💗 City → 🛍️ Shopping → 🍕 Dinner

Logistics Tip: Do not return to the hotel after Ba Na Hills. Head straight to the city to avoid unnecessary backtracking.

07:00 - Depart for Ba Na Hills

Leave directly after hotel breakfast.

08:30 - 15:30 - Ba Na Hills

Prioritize comfort. Focus on: Golden Bridge, Cable Car, French Village architecture, and viewpoints. Don't rush to see everything.

🍽️ Lunch: Four Seasons / French Village Buffet.

17:00 - Da Nang Pink Cathedral

Brief photo stop (20-30 mins max).

17:30 - Han Market Area

Relaxed shopping. If parents get tired, find a nearby cafe for them to rest.

19:00 - Dinner at Pizza 4P's

Advanced booking recommended. Perfect for sharing: 2 pizzas, salad, pasta, dessert.

20:15 - Evening Walk (Optional)

Han River walk / Dragon Bridge view. Skip and head to hotel if parents are tired. Return by 21:00.

DAY 03

10 Feb · Son Tra to Hoi An

🛕 Peace → 🌊 Ocean → 🏮 Ancient Town → ☕ Relax → 🍽️ Vietnamese Dinner

08:00 - Depart Hotel

Head to Son Tra Peninsula. Morning weather is usually pleasant.

08:30 - Linh Ung Pagoda & Son Tra

See the Lady Buddha and ocean views. Brief stop at Son Tra Viewpoint for photos.

10:00 - Travel to Hoi An

Approx 1-hour drive.

11:15 - Vietnamese Lunch

📍 Cơm Linh or similar local restaurant. Focus on chicken, fish, vegetables, and rice/noodles.

12:30 - Hoi An Ancient Town

Walk slowly. Old Streets, Lantern Shops, Japanese Covered Bridge. Don't chase every spot.

15:00 - Coffee & Rest Time

Dedicated rest time for parents in a comfortable, air-conditioned cafe with Vietnamese Coffee and desserts.

17:30 - Highly Recommended Dinner

📍 HOME Hoi An

Comfortable environment suitable for parents. Try: Spring Rolls, Bánh Xèo, local fish, chicken.

19:00 - Lantern Evening

Brief stroll along the lantern streets and riverside for family photos. Leave for Da Nang at 20:00 to rest early.

DAY 04

11 Feb · Slow Morning & Departure

🌊 Beach → ☕ Slow Morning → 🛍️ Last Shopping → ✈️ Home

08:00 - Hotel Breakfast & Beach

Leisurely breakfast. Stroll along My Khe Beach, enjoy the sea breeze without rushing.

10:30 - Last-minute Shopping

Pick up remaining coffee, souvenirs, or snacks.

12:45 - Light Lunch

📍 Bread & Butter. Keep it light before the flight.

13:30 - Transfer to Airport

Check out and head to Da Nang Airport early for a stress-free check-in for SQ173.

Souvenir Guide

Han Market (Food Focus)

  • Vietnamese Coffee - Local blends and drip filters.
  • Cashew Nuts - High quality and fresh.
  • Dried Mango & Fruits - Great for sharing.
  • Coconut Snacks - Crackers and candies.
  • Local Chocolate - Marou or similar brands.

Hoi An (Traditional)

  • Silk Lanterns - Hand-painted, fold flat for travel.
  • Bamboo Crafts - Bowls, coasters, decorative items.
  • Handmade Products - Embroidered bags, clothing.
  • Local Handicrafts - Pottery from Thanh Ha village.
